# Northwind Market Landscape Report (fixture)

Prepared for the Falk sample mandate "Five-market landscape report".

This report covers all five markets named in the agreement. Each has its own section:

1. Nordics - section 1, pages 2-5. Covers Denmark, Finland, Norway and Sweden.
2. DACH - section 2, pages 6-9. Covers Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
3. Benelux - section 3, pages 10-12. Covers Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg.
4. Iberia - section 4, pages 13-15. Covers Spain and Portugal.
5. Baltics - section 5, pages 16-18. Covers Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania.

All five markets named in the agreement (Nordics, DACH, Benelux, Iberia, Baltics) are covered.
